# Competitor — Everli

Marketplace: a personal shopper buys in the store of a partner chain. Extracted from the competitor table in [evidence.md](2026-10-10-desk-research-competitor-evidence.md) and the findings in [desk.md](../2026-10-10-desk-research-findings.md). The table row gives no source.

## Practice

| Dimension | What the desk research says |
|-----------|-----------------------------|
| Basket building | Depends on chosen store; catalogue mirrors the local partner's shelves |
| Substitution control | Human interaction: Personal Shopper calls or chats in real time from store if item is missing |
| Substitution notice | Real-time push notification or phone call while Personal Shopper is picking |
| Delivery windows | 1- or 2-hour windows, often with same-day slots available |
| At the door | Delivered by Personal Shopper using personal vehicle (service quality varies) |
