# Competitor — Tesco / Ocado

International reference (UK). Extracted from the competitor table in [evidence.md](2026-10-10-desk-research-competitor-evidence.md) and the findings in [desk.md](../2026-10-10-desk-research-findings.md). The table row gives no source.

## Practice

| Dimension | What the desk research says |
|-----------|-----------------------------|
| Basket building | Highly integrated (Ocado operates automated robotic warehouses rather than brick-and-mortar stores) |
| Substitution control | Predictive algorithms suggest best match; Ocado maintains very low missing/error rates (<1%) |
| Substitution notice | Immediate notification via digital receipt on driver's handheld device or in-app |
| Delivery windows | Precise delivery windows, down to 1-hour slots |
| At the door | Doorstep delivery; Ocado vans feature tri-temperature storage (ambient, chilled, frozen) |

## Related findings

| ID | Finding | Source | Confidence |
|----|---------|--------|------------|
| D14 | In a UK consumer survey, 19% (Ocado) and 26% (Tesco) of online shoppers had a substitution in their most recent shop. | S9 | medium, 2024 data |
| D16 | UK supermarkets in the survey let customers hand unwanted substitutes back to the driver for a refund. Not specific to Tesco or Ocado. | S9 | medium, 2024 data |
| D17 | Price rules for substitutes differ by retailer: some never charge more than the original item, others charge the price of the substitute. Not specific to Tesco or Ocado. | S9 | medium, 2024 data |
| D18 | Substitutions are communicated in different ways: by email or text before delivery, or by the driver at the door. Not specific to Tesco or Ocado. | S9 | medium, 2024 data |
