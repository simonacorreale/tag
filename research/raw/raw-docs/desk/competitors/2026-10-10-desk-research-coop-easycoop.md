# Competitor — Coop / EasyCoop

Second chain with its own online service. Extracted from the competitor table in [evidence.md](2026-10-10-desk-research-competitor-evidence.md) and the findings in [desk.md](../2026-10-10-desk-research-findings.md). The table row gives no source.

## Practice

| Dimension | What the desk research says |
|-----------|-----------------------------|
| Basket building | Catalogue focused on Coop own-brand and leading national brands |
| Substitution control | Handled at checkout: shopper can authorise picker to choose equivalent items |
| Substitution notice | Summary in digital receipt sent via email prior to delivery |
| Delivery windows | Flexible windows (1-hour slots with a surcharge) or standard 2-hour slots |
| At the door | Dedicated staff, delivery to the floor (free or with surcharge for upper floors without lift) |

## Related findings

| ID | Finding | Source | Confidence |

## Evidence from interviews

- **P2 Luca Volpi**: tried Coop (and Carrefour) before settling on Esselunga, after bad experiences. Felt the fresh products were less fresh. See [P2 note](../../../../notes/2026-10-25-interview-note-luca-volpi-p2.md).

## Signals

- In the table, Coop is the only direct chain that sends the substitution summary before delivery (see D18 and D19 in [desk.md](../2026-10-10-desk-research-findings.md)). No interview evidence yet.
