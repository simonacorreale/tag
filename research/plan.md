# Research plan — Redesigning grocery delivery

Master file for the research. One week, 2026-10-10 to 2026-10-16: research questions, desk research, 3 interviews, 2 observations. The brief is in [brief.md](brief.md). The map of all files is in [RADEME.md](RADEME.md).

## Goal

Design one feature that improves the online grocery delivery experience of a supermarket chain such as Esselunga. The basket is big, weekly and perishable. The feature must address one moment only: building the basket, handling substitutions, or receiving the order.

By the end of the week the research must say which moment to design for and which problem inside it is worth solving.

## Questions

| ID | Question | Answered by |
|----|----------|-------------|
| RQ1 | How do people plan and build a weekly grocery basket online? | Interviews, Observation 1 |
| RQ2 | What happens, practically and emotionally, when an item is unavailable? | Desk research, Interviews |
| RQ3 | What makes a substitution acceptable or unacceptable? | Desk research, Interviews |
| RQ4 | How do people choose a delivery window and what goes wrong on delivery day? | Interviews, Observation 2 |
| RQ5 | How do people judge freshness when they cannot pick the product themselves? | Interviews, Observation 2 |
| RQ6 | How do supermarket chains' online services handle the three moments today? | Desk research |

Working hypothesis: substitutions are the moment with the most evidence of friction (see [desk.md](raw/raw-docs/desk/2026-10-10-desk-research-findings.md)). It stays open until the synthesis on 2026-10-15.

## Desk research

Output: [desk.md](raw/raw-docs/desk/2026-10-10-desk-research-findings.md). Status: first pass done on 2026-10-10, needs review.

Remaining work, limited to one day:

1. Check the findings marked *to verify* against their original sources. Drop the ones that cannot be confirmed.
2. Walk through one order flow on one chain's own service (for example Esselunga a Casa), up to the payment step, and fill its row of the competitor table. This answers the main gap: how unavailable items are handled today.
3. Fill two more rows of the competitor table from help pages, without placing an order. Include at least one marketplace such as Everli.

Not in this week: a full competitor benchmark, and research on the gaps listed in desk.md beyond the service walkthrough.

## Interviews

Output: [interview.md](raw/raw-docs/interview/2026-10-10-interview-script.md). Status: guide ready, no interviews held.

- 3 semi-structured interviews, 30 to 40 minutes each, one per day.
- All three shop online for a large, recurring grocery basket at least twice a month.

| Code | Profile to recruit | Date |
|------|--------------------|------|
| P1 | Family with children, weekly order | 2026-10-12 |
| P2 | Single or couple, had a substitution or missing item in the last three months | 2026-10-13 |
| P3 | Someone who shops for an older relative, or who stopped ordering online | 2026-10-14 |

Each interview is written up in interview.md the same day.

## Observations

Output: `observation.md`, to be created. Status: planned.

| ID | What | How | Date |
|----|------|-----|------|
| O1 | A participant building a real weekly basket | Screen share or sitting beside them, thinking aloud, 30 minutes | 2026-10-12, with P1 after the interview |

## Out of scope for this week

- Concept and feature definition.
- Concept validation with participants.
- More than 3 interviews.

## Evidence rules

- Every finding records its source, its grade (primary, secondary or tertiary) and a confidence level.
- Figures from other markets (UK, US) are labelled as such and not presented as Italian data.
- Interview quotes are kept verbatim and anonymised with participant codes.
- Three interviews give qualitative evidence only. No percentages.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Recruiting 3 participants in two days | Start on 2026-10-10. Use personal contacts. Accept video calls. |
| No delivery to observe during the week | Fallback for O2 described above |
| Three interviews are too few to choose a moment with confidence | Combine with desk research. State the confidence level in the synthesis. |
| Most published evidence is from the UK and the US | Use the interviews and the service walkthrough to check what holds in Italy |
