# Desk research results — Everli

Marketplace: a personal shopper buys in the store of a partner chain. Built from `raw/raw-docs/desk/competitors/2026-10-10-desk-research-everli.md`, the competitor table in `raw/raw-docs/desk/competitors/2026-10-10-desk-research-competitor-evidence.md` and finding D20 in `raw/raw-docs/desk/2026-10-10-desk-research-findings.md`.

## Main result

Everli is the only service in the table where the shopper decides about a missing item during picking, by phone or chat. It is the opposite of a fixed upfront rule and answers the desk point that notice before delivery matters (D18, D19). The trade-off is variable delivery quality. No interview participant has used it.

| ID | Result | Evidence |

## What is not known

- What happens if the shopper does not answer the call or chat.
- Whether fresh products travel chilled in a personal vehicle.
- Fees and minimum spend.
- Any shopper's experience: no participant has used Everli.

## Limits

The table row has no source; D20 may be dated. No interview evidence.
