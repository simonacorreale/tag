# Desk research results — Coop / EasyCoop

Second chain with its own online service. Built from `raw/raw-docs/desk/competitors/2026-10-10-desk-research-coop-easycoop.md`, the competitor table in `raw/raw-docs/desk/competitors/2026-10-10-desk-research-competitor-evidence.md`, findings D20 and D21 in `raw/raw-docs/desk/2026-10-10-desk-research-findings.md`, and the results of P2.

## Main result

Coop is the only direct chain in the table that tells the shopper about substitutions before delivery, which is the practice the desk research favours (D18, D19). It also offers 1-hour windows for a surcharge. No interview participant uses it today; P2 tried it and left.

## Results by moment

### Building the basket

| ID | Result | Evidence |
|----|--------|----------|
| C-B1 | Catalogue focused on Coop own-brand and leading national brands. | evidence.md, no source |

### Handling substitutions

| ID | Result | Evidence |
|----|--------|----------|
| C-S1 | Handled at checkout: the shopper can authorise the picker to choose equivalent items. | evidence.md, no source |
| C-S2 | A summary of substitutions arrives by email in the digital receipt, before delivery. | evidence.md, no source |

### Receiving the order

| ID | Result | Evidence |
|----|--------|----------|
| C-R1 | Standard 2-hour windows, or 1-hour windows with a surcharge. | evidence.md, no source |
| C-R2 | Dedicated staff deliver to the floor; surcharge for upper floors without a lift. | evidence.md, no source |
| C-R3 | P2 tried Coop and found the fresh products less fresh than Esselunga's. | P2 Q3, Q19 |

### Market position

| ID | Result | Evidence |
|----|--------|----------|
| C-M1 | Among the top three in a consumer association survey (68 to 76 out of 100 across eight chains). | D21 |
| C-M2 | Coop is also reported among Everli's partner chains, so it is reachable through a marketplace too. | D20, may be dated |

## What is not known

- Whether the checkout authorisation lets the shopper refuse substitutions, or exclude single items.
- Whether the email summary arrives in time to change anything.
- Delivery fees and minimum spend.

## Limits

The table row has no source. Interview evidence is one participant's past experience, with no detail beyond freshness.
