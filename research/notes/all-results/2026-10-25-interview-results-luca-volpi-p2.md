# Interview results — P2 Luca Volpi

What the interview with P2 Luca Volpi shows, moment by moment. Built from two files only: the note `notes/2026-10-25-interview-note-luca-volpi-p2.md` and the transcript `raw/raw-docs/interview/luca-volpi/2026-10-25-interview-transcript-luca-volpi-p2.md`.

Every result names the guide question it comes from (Q1 to Q22). There is one recording only. Quotes are English translations of the Italian transcript.

## Participant

- P2 Luca Volpi. Household of four: himself, his wife and two young children. (Q1)
- UniCredit employee in Milan, with an employee discount on Esselunga. (Q3)
- Orders from Esselunga online about once a month, irregularly, and buys the whole shop online, fresh produce included. His wife usually places the order. (Q2, Q4)
- Tried Carrefour, Coop and a third service he cannot name, with bad experiences. (Q3)

Limit: the last order was his first one made with an AI assistant, so it is not his usual process.

## Main result

Building the basket is the moment that hurts, and finding products is the cause. He named search as the hardest part of ordering (Q5): too many unrelated results, a range too wide to scroll, and no way to tell a wrong search term from a product that is not sold. Substitutions barely register for him. The one thing he would change is delivery: he cannot commit days ahead to being home in a window (Q21).

## Results by moment

### Building the basket

| ID | Result | Evidence |
