# User stories — from the feature proposals

One story per feature in [2026-10-25-features-from-touchpoints.md](2026-10-25-features-from-touchpoints.md). Who, circumstance and value come from the interview notes. Each story is followed by its source line: feature · note · quote. The [strong] or [weak] mark is the feature's own.

Personas, from the notes:
- **P1, the shift worker without a car** — Federica Franco, 30, shops for herself and her partner. In Milan without a car she placed big stock-up orders of heavy items; she worked the morning shift. [Note](../2026-10-10-interview-note-federica-franco-p1.md).
- **P2, the father of two with shifting smart-working days** — Luca Volpi, shops for a family of four, orders the whole shop about once a month; his smart-working days change every week. [Note](../2026-10-25-interview-note-luca-volpi-p2.md).

No feature needed two stories, so none was split.

## Building the basket

1. **As the father of two with shifting smart-working days (P2), in the moment he types an item from the family list into the search bar and gets everything that contains the word, I want the product I searched for shown before products that only contain the word, so that I find the product I need without refining.**
   - Source: Search that puts the product first [strong] · P2, Q4 · "If you search for orange, you get orange juice, orange chocolate, then you have to refine until you find the product you need."

2. **As the father of two with shifting smart-working days (P2), facing pages of results for one product type, I want the results narrowed to the few that fit what I need, so that I can choose one without scrolling through pages.**
   - Source: Narrowing a long result list [strong] · P2, Q5 · "You don't feel like scrolling through four pages of detergents to choose the one you need."

3. **As the father of two with shifting smart-working days (P2), when a search for an unusual product such as lemon juice returns nothing useful, I want to be told whether the product is not sold or my search term did not match, so that I know whether it's me who wrote the wrong word or the product really isn't there.**
   - Source: Telling "not sold" from "not found" [weak] · P2, Q9 · "You never understand whether it's you who didn't write the right word or whether the product really isn't there."

4. **As the shift worker without a car (P1), when she looks online for a product that has just been launched in stores and cannot find it, I want to be told when it becomes available online, so that I get it while I still want it, not months later.**
   - Source: Notice when a new product arrives online [weak] · P1, Q9 · "Some new ice creams had come out […] And Esselunga got them at the end of summer, when nobody was eating them any more."

5. **As the shift worker without a car (P1), when she adds loose fruit and vegetables and has to enter the quantity, I want to state the amount in terms I understand, so that I understand what I am ordering.**
   - Source: Clear quantities for loose produce [strong] · P1, Q21 · "When you have to buy fresh fruit and vegetables and you had to enter the quantity, it was really awkward, you could not understand anything."

6. **As the shift worker without a car (P1), while browsing the catalogue pages in the app, I want each product recognisable at a glance, so that shopping from the app is no longer uncomfortable.**
   - Source: Recognisable products while browsing [strong] · P1, Q21 · "It is really uncomfortable to shop from the apps, the products are hard to see on the pages."

7. **As the shift worker without a car (P1), while building a big stock-up order of heavy items, I want to see which offers apply to my basket, including those that remove the delivery fee, so that I get free delivery on the big order.**
   - Source: Free-delivery offers visible while ordering [weak] · P1, Q4 · "I usually always checked whether there was an offer like, to give you an example, Mutti: buy 20 euros of Mutti products and you get free delivery."

8. **As the father of two with shifting smart-working days (P2), at the end of a session of more than 20 minutes, before confirming, I want to check the whole basket quickly, so that I can drop what I added out of enthusiasm and don't really need, without it taking long.**
   - Source: Quick basket review before confirming [weak] · P2, Q7 · "Sometimes you add things out of enthusiasm and then realise you don't really need them. So yes, it takes time, it's not quick."

## Paying and confirming

9. **As the shift worker without a car (P1), at the payment step of her usual Esselunga order, I want to pay with meal vouchers, so that I can use my meal vouchers without switching to another service.**
   - Source: Meal vouchers accepted [weak] · P1, Q3 · "I used Glovo a couple of times because there were offers and I could pay with meal vouchers, but usually Esselunga online […] because you cannot use meal vouchers on the Esselunga app."

10. **As the shift worker without a car (P1), when she has to tick the box authorising substitutions to place the order, I want to mark the few items that must never be substituted while allowing the rest, so that the items I pay a lot for, or care about the taste of, are left out rather than replaced.**
    - Source: Protected items: never substitute [strong] · P1, Q15 · "If I pay a lot for it, I do not accept that you substitute it. I would rather you leave it out and take the price off what I paid."

11. **As the shift worker without a car (P1), two or three days before the Friday afternoon she is free after the morning shift, I want to hold that delivery window without completing the basket yet, so that I get the window I want.**
    - Source: Choose the window before the basket [weak] · P1, Q16 · "Say I wanted it delivered on Friday afternoon, because I worked the morning shift […] Usually either I ordered early or I never got the window I wanted."
    - Limit: the value traces to getting the wanted window. The notes do not show that ordering the whole basket early bothered her.

## Between order and delivery

12. **As the father of two with shifting smart-working days (P2), when he books a window days ahead without knowing whether he will be home, I want the order delivered, chilled, when nobody is home, so that I don't have to be sure that in two days I can stay home waiting for the shopping.**
    - Source: Delivery without anyone at home [strong] · P2, Q21 · "The hardest thing I find is being sure that in two days, from four to six, I can stay home waiting for the shopping." · P2, Q21 · "A sort of refrigerated locker."

13. **As the father of two with shifting smart-working days (P2), when his family's week changes after he has booked a window, I want to move the delivery window, so that the delivery falls when I am actually home.**
    - Source: Change the window after ordering [weak] · P2, Q21 · "Every week my family has something different and I have to change my smart-working days […] Maybe Wednesday afternoon I'll be home, but maybe not."

## Receiving the order

14. **As the shift worker without a car (P1), when she places an order and her chosen brand has already run out in the store that picks it, I want to be stopped from ordering an item already out of stock, so that I don't have to accept the basic Esselunga product as a substitute, the stress I had every time I did a shop.**
    - Source: Stock that matches the picking store [strong] · P1, Q5 · "If that brand was not there, you had to accept the basic Esselunga one as a substitute, paying the price difference of course. That is the stress I had every time I did a shop." · P1, Q21 · "As items run out […] you take them off the app."
    - Contradiction carried over: P1 says the order is picked in the nearest store (Q21); the desk note says Esselunga controls freshness from a central warehouse.

15. **As the shift worker without a car (P1), when an item she paid for is missing from the delivered order, I want to be charged only for what is delivered, so that I don't have to wait for the money back after I have paid.**
    - Source: No charge for items not delivered [strong] · P1, Q21 · "I do not want you to substitute it, or to have to wait for you to give me the money back after I have paid because it is not there."
