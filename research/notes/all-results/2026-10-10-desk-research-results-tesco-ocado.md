# Desk research results — Tesco / Ocado

International reference (UK), not available in Italy. Built from `raw/raw-docs/desk/competitors/2026-10-10-desk-research-tesco-ocado.md`, the competitor table in `raw/raw-docs/desk/competitors/2026-10-10-desk-research-competitor-evidence.md` and findings D14, D16, D17, D18 in `raw/raw-docs/desk/2026-10-10-desk-research-findings.md`.

## Main result

Tesco and Ocado are the only services in the desk research with measured substitution rates: 19% (Ocado) and 26% (Tesco) of shoppers had a substitution in their last shop (D14). Ocado's model, automated warehouses and predictive best match, is the reference for stock accuracy, the upstream fix P1 asks for. The UK practices on refunds and notice (D16 to D18) are from a survey of UK supermarkets in general, not these two only.

## Contradictions

| Desk says | Desk also says |
|-----------|----------------|
| Ocado missing or error rates below 1% (evidence.md) | 19% of Ocado shoppers had a substitution in their last shop (D14) |

The two may measure different things (errors vs substitutions). Both versions are kept; the table row gives no source.

## What is not known

- Which of D16 to D18 apply to Tesco and Ocado specifically.
- How Tesco, which picks from stores, differs from Ocado on substitutions.

## Limits

UK market, not comparable with Italy without care. The table row has no source. No interview evidence.
