# Feature proposals — from the touchpoints

Minimum features the service could offer at each touchpoint in [2026-10-25-touchpoints-interview-desk.md](2026-10-25-touchpoints-interview-desk.md). Proposals, not decisions: the choice of moment and feature stays with the researcher.

Note codes as in the touchpoints file: **P1** = [Federica Franco note](../2026-10-10-interview-note-federica-franco-p1.md), **P2** = [Luca Volpi note](../2026-10-25-interview-note-luca-volpi-p2.md).

**Strength:**
- **[strong]** — the participant named this moment as the hardest part (Q5) or as the thing to change (Q21), or both participants describe it.
- **[weak]** — one passing mention, an ambiguous moment, or a moment with no friction in the quote.

Only interview quotes anchor a feature. Touchpoints that come only from desk notes describe what competitors offer; using them would be competitor scanning, so they hold no feature (see Dropped).

## Building the basket

| feature | touchpoint it answers | evidence (note + quote) | what it does, one line |
|---------|-----------------------|-------------------------|------------------------|
| Search that puts the product first [strong] | Cercare un prodotto con la barra di ricerca | P2, Q4: "If you search for orange, you get orange juice, orange chocolate, then you have to refine until you find the product you need." P2, Q5 names search as the hardest part. | Returns the product searched for before products that only contain the word. |
| Narrowing a long result list [strong] | Scorrere i risultati per sceglierne uno | P2, Q5: "You don't feel like scrolling through four pages of detergents to choose the one you need." | Reduces many results to the few that fit what the shopper needs. |
| Telling "not sold" from "not found" [weak] | Non trovare un prodotto cercato (ambiguo) | P2, Q9: "You never understand whether it's you who didn't write the right word or whether the product really isn't there." | Says whether the product is not in the range or the search term did not match. |
| Notice when a new product arrives online [weak] | Cercare un prodotto appena lanciato | P1, Q9: "If you see a product that has just come out, you do not find it straight away. You have to wait a few more months." | Tells the shopper when a product they looked for becomes available online. |
| Clear quantities for loose produce [strong] | Inserire la quantità di frutta e verdura sfusa | P1, Q21: "When you have to buy fresh fruit and vegetables and you had to enter the quantity, it was really awkward, you could not understand anything." | Lets the shopper state how much loose produce they want in terms they understand. |
| Recognisable products while browsing [strong] | Guardare i prodotti nelle pagine dell'app | P1, Q21: "It is really uncomfortable to shop from the apps, the products are hard to see on the pages." | Makes each product recognisable at a glance in the catalogue. |
| Free-delivery offers visible while ordering [weak] | Consultare le promozioni del servizio | P1, Q4: "I checked whether there was some offer […] buy 20 euros of Mutti products and you get free delivery." · P2, Q6: "I look at which fish is on offer." | Shows which offers apply to the basket being built, including those that remove the delivery fee. |
| Quick basket review before confirming [weak] | Rivedere il carrello prima di chiudere l'ordine | P2, Q7: "You'd usually look through the basket and try to spot the things that stood out, also because sometimes you add things out of enthusiasm." | Lets the shopper check the whole basket quickly before confirming. |

## Receiving the order

| feature | touchpoint it answers | evidence (note + quote) | what it does, one line |
|---------|-----------------------|-------------------------|------------------------|
| Stock that matches the picking store [strong] | Trovare un prodotto sostituito | P1, Q5: "If that brand was not there, you had to accept the basic Esselunga one as a substitute." · P1, Q21: "As items run out […] you take them off the app." | Stops the shopper ordering an item that is already out of stock. |
| No charge for items not delivered [strong] | Trovare un prodotto mancante o rovinato | P1, Q21: "I do not want you to substitute it, or to have to wait for you to give me the money back after I have paid because it is not there." · P1, Q15: "I would rather you leave it out and take the price off what I paid." | Charges only for what is delivered, with no refund to wait for. |
| List of what was substituted [weak] | Trovare un prodotto sostituito (ambiguo) | P2, Q11: "If I receive a product different from the one I bought, I often don't even notice." · P1, Q11: "I do not remember with which item because too much time has passed." | Tells the shopper which items in the order are substitutes. |

## Count and notes

- **Features proposed:** 16. Strong: 8. Weak: 8.
- **Considered and dropped:** 14.
- **By moment:**
  - building the basket, payment included: 9 features, 4 strong;
  - substitutions: 3 features, 2 strong (protected items, stock that matches the store);
  - receiving the order, delivery window included: 4 features, 2 strong (no charge for missing items, delivery without anyone at home).
- **Single source:** each strong feature rests on one participant. The touchpoints file has no feature that both participants ask for.
- **Contradiction that affects a feature:** "Stock that matches the picking store" assumes the order is picked in a store (P1, Q21). The desk note says Esselunga controls freshness from a central warehouse. Both versions are in the touchpoints file.
