# Redesigning grocery delivery
Design a feature that improves the online grocery delivery experience of a supermarket chain such as Esselunga — not restaurant food delivery. The basket is big, weekly and perishable: substitutions when items are unavailable, delivery windows, freshness, recurring purchases. Pick one moment: building the basket, handling substitutions, or receiving the order.
